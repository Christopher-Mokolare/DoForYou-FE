DoForYou Terms & Conditions

Community-based service connecting task posters with helpers

DoForYou is a community-based service that connects task posters with individuals ("helpers") who can assist with everyday tasks for a fair rate.

By submitting this form, you confirm the following:

You understand that DoForYou is a connector only and does not vet, insure, or guarantee helpers.

You acknowledge that all task payments are made upfront to allow matching, and if no suitable helper is found, you will receive a refund.

You release DoForYou (and its founder, TA Mokoena) from any liability, injury, damages, or loss resulting from your interaction with a helper.

You agree not to post illegal, abusive, or unsafe tasks.

This is a trust-based platform — please use it respectfully.

Platform Fees

DoForYou charges a 15% service fee on every paid task to cover platform operations, coordination, and communication. This fee is included in the amount you submit when posting a task.

Example:

If you post a task for R200:

  • R30 will go to the platform
  • R170 will go to the helper

Safety Guidelines

For Runners (Task Helpers)

  • Be polite, honest, and punctual.
  • Never pressure anyone into giving you more money.
  • Only claim tasks you can actually complete.
  • Keep a clear record of what you did (photos, receipts, etc.) in case there's any issue.
  • Respect the poster's privacy and property.
  • If you feel unsafe or unsure, notify the DoForYou team immediately.

For Posters (Task Senders)

  • Don't share sensitive info until you're sure you trust the runner.
  • Use safe public meeting spots when possible.
  • Keep communication inside the WhatsApp thread or group where it's visible.
  • Pay the committed budget only once you've posted through the form — no direct deals.
  • If anything feels off, contact Ashwin or the DoForYou admin.

For Task Poster and Task Runners

  • No illegal activity, harassment, scams, or dishonesty of any kind.
  • We are not liable for loss or damages — but we'll do our best to support fairness.
  • You use the platform at your own risk — please be kind, safe, and responsible.
